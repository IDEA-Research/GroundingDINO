Device: Philips IntelliVue MP20/MP30 Patient Monitor

[Visual Identification]
- **Layout**: "Philips" logo at bottom center.
- **Key Feature**: A vertical stack of large numbers on the RIGHT side of the screen.
- **Color Coding**: The values are strictly color-coded.

[Visibility Rules]
- **Glare/Obstruction**: If a number is covered by glare or an object, return null.
- **Empty Values**: If the value is "?", "--", or blank, return null.
- **Distinguish Digits**: Do not confuse "7" with "1" if blurry. If unsure, return null.

[Extraction Rules]
Extract the following medical values based on the **Right-Side Vertical Stack** and **Color**.

--- The "Big Three" (Right Side Stack) ---
Look at the large numeric blocks stacked vertically on the right side.

1. **Heart Rate (Top Block)**
   - key: `heart_rate`
   - Color: **GREEN**
   - Location: Usually the top-most large number.
   - Label: "HR" or "Pulse".

2. **SpO2 (Middle Block)**
   - key: `spo2`
   - Color: **BLUE** or **CYAN** (Light Blue)
   - Location: Usually in the middle of the stack, below HR.
   - Label: "SpO2" or "%".

3. **Respiration Rate (Bottom Block)**
   - key: `respiration_rate`
   - Color: **YELLOW** or **ORANGE/WHITE**
   - Location: Usually the bottom-most large number in the stack.
   - Label: "Resp", "RR", or "awRR".
