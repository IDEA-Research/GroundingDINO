Device: Somanetics INVOS Cerebral/Somatic Oximeter

[Visual Identification]
- **Logo**: "SOMANETICS" or "INVOS" logo usually at the top.
- **Layout**: Screen is distinctly divided into Left ("L") and Right ("R") sections.
- **Main Feature**: Very large white or blue numbers representing rSO2.

[Visibility & Safety Rules]
- **Sensor Disconnection**: If a side shows "--", blank, blue dots, or "OFF", return null for that side.
- **Glare**: If the "L" or "R" label is visible but the number is obscured by white glare, return null.

[Extraction Rules]
Extract the rSO2 values based on the **"L" (Left)** and **"R" (Right)** indicators on the screen.

1. **Left Side Sensor (L)**
   - **Anchor**: Look for the large letter **"L"** or "Left".
   - `rso2_left`: The **BIG** number associated with the "L" indicator. (Current value).
   - `rso2_left_avg`: The **SMALLER** number displayed directly below the big "L" number. (Often labeled "Avg" or "BL").

2. **Right Side Sensor (R)**
   - **Anchor**: Look for the large letter **"R"** or "Right".
   - `rso2_right`: The **BIG** number associated with the "R" indicator. (Current value).
   - `rso2_right_avg`: The **SMALLER** number displayed directly below the big "R" number. (Often labeled "Avg" or "BL").
